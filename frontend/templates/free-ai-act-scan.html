Frequently Asked Questions

Is the scan really free?

Yes. The first scan is free, unlimited in scope, and needs no signup. Paid plans are only for recurring scans and team features.

Is my code safe?

Scanning runs against your files. Your source code only leaves your machine if you explicitly upload it, and we never store or share it.

Do I have to be in the EU?

No. The EU AI Act applies to any company whose AI output is used in the EU, wherever the company is based. Use the scanner from anywhere.

Does the score prove compliance?

The scan proves which technical requirements your code already satisfies and which gaps remain. It is not legal advice and does not replace a formal conformity assessment.
