About AI Compliance Shield

AI Compliance Shield is an independent compliance tool that helps software companies check whether their AI systems meet the requirements of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). Enforcement of the core transparency rules began on August 2, 2026, and many companies are not yet certain where they stand.

We built the tool to answer one question in minutes: is my AI compliant, and if not, what do I fix first? Instead of a manual audit across dozens of regulatory articles, our scanner maps your codebase and configuration against a requirements engine and returns a clear 0–100% score with a prioritised remediation plan.

We serve a broad range of companies — from startups shipping their first AI feature to established SaaS teams selling into Europe. We operate from India and service clients across the EU and worldwide.

Our output is a technical, evidence-based check — not legal advice. We always encourage teams to validate their final position with qualified counsel, but we get them to a solid, documented starting point fast.
